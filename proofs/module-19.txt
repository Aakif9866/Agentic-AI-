Parallel nodes each produce partial results that must be combined into a final answer.  
A reducer gathers these pieces and merges them safely without losing data.  
Example: three nodes count visited users, then reducer sums 120, 85, and 95.  
The sum 300 becomes the correct total across the whole graph.
